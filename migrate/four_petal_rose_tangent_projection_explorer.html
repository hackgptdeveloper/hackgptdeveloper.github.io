Point B moves around the four-lobed polar rose r = a cos(2θ). At each point, the tangent line is drawn and the perpendicular foot D of a fixed point C is constructed. The cyan curve is the complete trajectory of D while θ swings between −π and +π.

Geometry and motion

θ is animated by reflected linear motion: −π → +π → −π. A four-petal rose is already fully traced by any interval of length π, but the full swing is retained so the reversing motion is visible.

For tangent direction v = (x′,y′), the perpendicular foot of C onto the tangent at B is
D = B + v · ((C−B)·v)/(v·v).
