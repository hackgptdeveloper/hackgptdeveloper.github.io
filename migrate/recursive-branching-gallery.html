Recursive branching curves

The supplied image is best described as a curved fractal tree: smaller branching structures repeat the shape of larger ones. “Recursive branching tree” is also a useful search term. The screenshot alone does not determine an exact generating equation or a unique named construction.

These are branching structures, not necessarily single parametrized curves. The gallery separates standard names from descriptive variations. All previews are finite-depth illustrative constructions, not identifications of the original video's algorithm.

How the drawings work

Each branch produces shorter descendants. The length ratio controls how quickly branches shrink; the angle controls their spread; depth controls how many generations are drawn. Curved versions additionally turn along each branch. Repeating the branching rule indefinitely gives the idealized construction; a browser can display only a finite approximation. Self-similar branching does not, by itself, establish a particular fractal dimension.
