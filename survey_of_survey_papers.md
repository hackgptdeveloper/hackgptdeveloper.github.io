## Broader survey collections

For continuous tracking rather than a fixed list, these are especially useful:

- **arXiv cs.LG recent listings** include explicitly tagged survey papers across machine learning; for example, recent entries include railway anomaly detection and FPGA-enabled Earth-observation ML surveys. [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/current)
- **LLM Survey Papers on arXiv** aggregates recent LLM surveys, including prompt injection, alignment, RAG, data strategy, memory compression, RLVR, benchmark compression, and tutoring. [arxiv.deeppaper](https://arxiv.deeppaper.ai/surveys/llm)
- **NiuTrans/ABigSurveyOfLLMs** is a large curated GitHub collection of more than 150 LLM surveys, organized by topics such as pretraining, prompting, data, evaluation, efficiency, and agents. [github](https://github.com/NiuTrans/ABigSurveyOfLLMs)

## How to filter for “detailed” surveys

When selecting papers, prioritize ones that:

- Provide an explicit **taxonomy** rather than only a chronological literature review.
- Include comparison tables of methods, datasets, benchmarks, and metrics.
- Cover both **training-time** and **deployment/inference-time** concerns.
- Discuss limitations, threat models, failure modes, and open problems.
- Are published in a peer-reviewed venue or have a mature, repeatedly revised arXiv version.

For your interests in cloud systems, identity, and security, the strongest immediate reads are likely the **prompt-injection SoK**, **LLM unlearning survey**, **privacy-preserving ML review**, **distributed LLM training survey**, and **model-compression survey**.
