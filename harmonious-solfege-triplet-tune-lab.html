Harmonious Triplet Tune Lab

These are melodic three-note cells—not mood labels. Each button plays a looping tune built around one solfège triplet in C major: Do = C, Re = D, Mi = E, Fa = F, So = G, La = A, and Ti = B.

Every triplet has been selected for a clear melodic relationship: stepwise motion, outlines of a familiar triad, or a consonant skip followed by a smooth return. A gentle I–vi–IV–V accompaniment changes around the melody, so the notes join harmoniously rather than sounding like isolated beeps.

Press Play on a melodic triplet. Each example loops with piano-like melody and soft chord accompaniment.

All examples use the major scale, though individual notes may appear in neighboring octaves for singable contour. Choose one triplet, listen to the melody, then use it as a motif in a larger composition.
